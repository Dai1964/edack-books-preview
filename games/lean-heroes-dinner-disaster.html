For grown-ups

DMAIC in one minute

DMAIC is five steps for fixing a problem so it stays fixed. Here they are with the dinner disaster:

  • Define: say the problem in one sentence. "Dinner was 45 minutes late."
  • Measure: count what really happened, like how many dishes got burnt.
  • Analyse: ask "Why?" again and again until you reach the root cause, not just the first answer.
  • Improve: try a better way, like a prep checklist or a timer, and check it works.
  • Control: make the fix stick. Write the new steps down and keep checking them.

Why it matters

These are the same five steps real factories, hospitals and offices use to fix problems. Here your child practises them in a kitchen.

The Feynman check

Can you explain the five steps to your child using something from your own home, like a messy bedroom or a late school run? Then you've got it.

Fifteen minutes together, and you both get a way to break down problems.
