        For grown-ups

        SIPOC in one minute

        SIPOC is a one-page picture of a process, in five columns:

        • Suppliers: who gives us what we need.
        • Inputs: what we need.
        • Process: the main steps we do.
        • Outputs: what comes out.
        • Customers: who gets it.

        You fill it in before you change anything. Then everyone agrees what the process is, where it starts and where it ends.

        Why real businesses use it

        Improvement teams often draw a SIPOC at the start of a project, in the Define step of DMAIC. It shows the edges of the problem. It also catches the forgotten input or the forgotten customer before they cause trouble. It works the same for a factory line, a hospital ward or a school lunch queue.
